The People's Calculator

Tired of apps cluttered with ads and hidden subscriptions, I decided to build we11tex. My goal is to keep this tool free, ad-free, and folk—built for drivers, not for dashboards. We rely on the honesty and support of the driving community. If it helps you earn more, feel free to fuel the code.

Why now

Platforms change fees and policies faster than most people can update a spreadsheet. I shipped we11tex in that gap: one straight screen with real inputs, no account wall, so you can sanity-check a shift before tax season—or before taking one more fare. If the numbers save you time, that is the whole point.

No data tracking. Total privacy.
